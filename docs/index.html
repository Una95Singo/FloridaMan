The loop

1 · Draft. A storm event triggers the watch agent, which drafts an exposure hypothesis.

2 · QC. A QC agent validates mechanics only — grounding, arithmetic, freshness, completeness. One blocker stops the run.

3 · Expert. The senior expert is asked one question: "where am I wrong?" Corrections enter the trace.

4 · Publish. The QC-passed, expert-reviewed hypothesis publishes with its full trace.

5 · Score. Reality is the audit: predictions carry timestamped kill conditions and are scored after the event.
