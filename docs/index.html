A zero-based agentic product for insurance-linked securities

Work can be done differently.

FloridaMan is a live, public demonstration that agentic workflows can replace — not merely accelerate — how ILS professionals deal with hurricanes. Autonomous agents watch storms, reason about which catastrophe bonds are exposed, and publish traceable, falsifiable hypotheses. A human expert judges; reality scores the predictions.

1Draft
A storm event triggers the watch agent, which drafts an exposure hypothesis.
2QC
A QC agent validates mechanics only — grounding, arithmetic, freshness, completeness.
3Expert
The senior expert is asked one question: "where am I wrong?" Corrections enter the trace.
4Publish
The QC-passed, expert-reviewed hypothesis publishes with its full trace.
5Score
Reality is the audit: predictions carry timestamped kill conditions and are scored after the event.

There is no dashboard. Nothing here starts with a human opening an app — agents trigger on events, the trace arrives for judgment, and silence is correct: no trigger, no output. Hypotheses are indicative ranges, never point estimates, and never secondary-market pricing.
