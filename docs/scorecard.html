How scoring works

1 · Hypothesis errors — expected, scored, learned from. A disproven call is the job working: the falsification conditions said what would prove it wrong, and reality did. Each gets: what was predicted, what happened, which assumption failed.

2 · Mechanical errors — target zero. Wrong arithmetic, stale sources, ungrounded claims. Each is recorded as a defect with its fix (run 001's two blockers are already logged this way).

3 · Calibration. Every confidence level is recorded alongside its outcome. As storms accumulate: when the agent says "medium," how often is it right?

4 · Next version. Every scorecard ends with what the next version of the agent should do differently — the roadmap earns its items here, not in a planning doc.
