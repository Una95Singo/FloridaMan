# Geographic source
Natural Earth 1:50m land polygons, regional feature subset.
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson
Retrieved: 2026-10-10. The checked-in bytes are the reproducible asset version.
License: public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Processing: retain features with vertices between 102W and 65W, 10N and 40N. Coordinates unchanged.
Coastal bands are cartographic decoration, not measured bathymetry or hazard data.
Storm positions, forecast paths and cone in the isolated replay are synthetic and visibly labelled.
No map tile, font or rendering service is requested at runtime.
