## Hypothesis (indicative, not marks)

Base case (Cat 2 landfall late Friday/early Saturday near the AL–FL
border, weakening under shear): still no attachment in the universe is
*likely* threatened — but the margin has narrowed materially since run
002. The two sub-$500m attachments (Hestia B $405m, First Coast B
$390m) sit in the central landfall scenario; a direct hit at or above
forecast intensity over populated Panhandle coastline makes attachment
plausible, where run 002 could only call it conditional. Bayou Re A
($1.05bn) needs a severe, surge-heavy event. If the storm reaches Cat 3
and holds it to landfall (against the shear forecast), re-examine all
sub-$2bn attachments urgently and promote Kilimanjaro II to the lead.

Sensitivity order: Hestia Re B, First Coast Re B, Bayou Re A,
Kilimanjaro II (tail) — then a large gap to everything else.

Confidence: MEDIUM (track confidence higher than run 002; landfall
intensity hinges entirely on shear timing, which models split on).

Aggregate erosion: unchanged from run 002 — 2026 season still
record-quiet, retentions most likely intact; Isaias faces essentially
full attachments on aggregate covers.

  sub-$2bn attachments; Kilimanjaro II industry-loss becomes the lead
